# PageCheck launch pack

The goal of the first 7 days: **50 real audits and 5 replies**, via direct outreach.
Everything else (Reddit, Product Hunt, SEO) waits until the tool has proven it gets used.

Tool: **https://pagecheck-946f.onrender.com**

## Daily loop (30–40 min)

1. Pick 10 local businesses (Google Maps: dentist, gym, restaurant, clinic, agency, store).
2. Run each site through PageCheck.
3. Note the score and the single worst fix.
4. Send the matching email from `OUTREACH-EMAILS.md`.
5. Log it in `TRACKER.csv`.

Stop at 10–15 per day. Personalized, not bulk.

## Rules that keep you out of trouble

- **Canada's anti-spam law (CASL):** cold commercial email needs consent. The safest
  route is an address that is **conspicuously published** for the business role
  (e.g. `info@` or a contact page) and a message relevant to that role. Always
  include your name, business location, and a working unsubscribe line (footer
  template is in `OUTREACH-EMAILS.md`). Stop immediately if asked.
- **Never mass-send.** Ten personalised emails beat 200 identical ones.
- **No fake urgency, no "I noticed your site is broken" shaming.** Lead with the
  free value.
- **One follow-up, five business days later.** Then stop.
