# 20-second screen recording

Record on the phone or with a screen recorder at 1080×1920 or 1920×1080.
No voice needed — captions only. Post to Reels, TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn.

## Shots

| Time | Shot | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | The PageCheck hero with the URL bar | "Your page is losing visitors." |
| 3–8 s | Type `sixside.onrender.com`, press Audit | "Paste a link." |
| 8–12 s | Score ring counts to 82 | "Get a score in seconds." |
| 12–17 s | "Fix these first" — scroll the three fixes | "See the top 3 fixes." |
| 17–20 s | Click "Show all 30 checks" | "Free. No sign-up. pagecheck-946f.onrender.com" |

## Rules

- Show the URL on screen at the end, large.
- Keep the phone UI clean — no notifications.
- One idea per video. Don't explain all 30 checks.
- Post the same video to three platforms, then make a second one next week.

## Alternate hook (test both)

> "I audited 10 local business sites. Eight had the same three problems."
> Then show three reports, then the tool.
