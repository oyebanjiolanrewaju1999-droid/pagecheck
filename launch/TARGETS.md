## Qualify before emailing

- Has a website (not only Instagram).
- Site looks dated, slow, or mobile-awkward — or the score is below 75.
- Has a public email on the site or a contact form.
- Not a franchise with a corporate marketing team.

## Personalise with one line

Pull one concrete issue from the audit:

- "No description for Google"
- "Shared links have no image"
- "No mobile viewport — the page looks tiny on phones"
- "No clear call to action above the fold"

That one line is the difference between a reply and the bin.
