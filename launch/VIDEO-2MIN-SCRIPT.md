**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical, 30 fps, phone at eye level.
**Length:** 1:55–2:05. About 270 spoken words at a natural pace.
**Structure:** hook (face) → problem (face) → what it is (face) → demo (screen) → proof (face) → CTA (face).

Do not add music. Record clean audio; music gets added in the edit.

## Recording checklist

- **Light:** face a window, not the window behind you. If it's dark, one lamp at
  eye level is enough.
- **Framing:** camera at eye level, top of head near the top edge, eyes on the
  upper third. Leave the bottom third clear — captions go there.
- **Audio:** phone mic at 30–40 cm, or earbuds with a mic. Record in a small room
  with soft furnishings; avoid kitchens and bathrooms.
- **Teleprompter:** use CapCut's teleprompter, or put the script just behind the
  camera lens. Don't read off the screen — it shows.
- **Takes:** record three full takes. Pause for one second between sections so
  cuts are easy.
- **Hands:** keep them out of frame unless you're pointing at the screen.
- **Don't say "um, so, basically".** If a line goes wrong, stop, breathe, restart
  that line — not the whole take.
